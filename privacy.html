2. What data I collect

2.1 What you type into the contact form

The contact form on this site is the only place where you are asked to give personal data. It collects:

  • Name — required
  • Email address — required, so I can reply
  • Phone number — optional
  • Website address — optional
  • Your message — required, and whatever you choose to put in it

Please do not send sensitive personal information through this form. Under Section 3(l) of RA 10173 that includes race, ethnic origin, marital status, age, colour, religious, philosophical or political affiliations, health, education, genetic or sexual life, any proceeding for an offence, and government-issued identifiers such as TIN, SSS, GSIS, PhilHealth, passport or driver's licence numbers. I do not need any of it, I do not ask for it, and this form is not the right channel for it.

2.2 What is collected automatically

  • Your IP address, which is used to rate-limit the form against abuse and is stored alongside your enquiry
  • The date and time your message was submitted
  • Standard server log data kept by my hosting provider and by the security proxy in front of this site — typically the page requested, browser user agent, referring page and response status

2.3 What I deliberately do not collect

This site has no analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels, no user accounts, no logins and no payment processing. It sets no cookies of its own, and it does not build a profile of you or track you across other websites. The only third-party cookies involved are described in section 7.

3. Why I process your data, and my lawful basis

Section 12 of RA 10173 requires a lawful basis for processing personal information. Each purpose below is matched to the ground I rely on.

To read your message and reply to you

Your consent, given when you submit the form — Sec. 12(a). Where your enquiry concerns a possible engagement, also the steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract — Sec. 12(b).

To keep a record of the enquiry so I can follow up and know what was discussed

My legitimate interests in running a professional practice and maintaining an accurate record of client communications — Sec. 12(f).

To stop spam and abuse — rate limiting, the hidden bot trap on the form, reCAPTCHA and server security

My legitimate interests in keeping the site secure, available and usable — Sec. 12(f).

To comply with a legal obligation, or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim

Compliance with a legal obligation — Sec. 12(c) — and my legitimate interests — Sec. 12(f).

I do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling. I do not sell, rent, trade or lease it, and I do not add you to a mailing list or send you marketing you did not ask for.

4. Who your data is shared with

I do not share your data with anyone for their own purposes. It is handled by the service providers this website runs on, each acting as a Personal Information Processor or otherwise receiving data as a technical consequence of serving the page:

  • My web hosting provider — stores this website, the enquiry record and the server logs, and sends the notification email to my inbox.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — content delivery and security proxy sitting in front of the site. It receives your IP address and request metadata for every page you load.
  • Google LLC — reCAPTCHA — verifies that the form was submitted by a person, not a bot. It receives your IP address and information about your browser and interaction with the page, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms.
  • Google LLC — Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site. Loading them discloses your IP address to Google.
  • Tailwind Labs, Inc. — serves the CSS framework used to style this site, again disclosing your IP address as a technical consequence of the request.

Beyond these, I will disclose personal data only where I am required to by Philippine law, a court order, or a lawful request from the National Privacy Commission or another government authority — and where I am permitted to tell you, I will.

5. Transfers outside the Philippines

Some of the providers listed above are based outside the Philippines, and your data may be processed or stored on servers in other countries, including the United States. Section 21 of RA 10173 makes me accountable for personal data under my control even after it has been transferred to a third party, whether that party is here or abroad. I use providers that publish privacy commitments and maintain security measures comparable to those required under the Act, and I remain responsible to you for how your data is handled.

6. How long your data is kept

Personal data is kept only as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, as required by Section 11(e) of RA 10173.

  • Enquiry records — your name, email, phone, website, message, IP address and timestamp are kept for 24 months from our last contact, then deleted.
  • If we go on to work together — the enquiry becomes part of the client record and is kept for the duration of the engagement plus any period required for tax, accounting or legal purposes under Philippine law.
  • Rate-limiting records — a counter keyed to a one-way hash of your IP address, which expires automatically about one hour after your last submission.
  • Server logs — kept by the hosting provider and the security proxy under their own retention schedules, not mine.

You can ask me to delete your enquiry sooner — see section 8.

7. Cookies and affiliate links

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to on my side.

The Google reCAPTCHA widget on the contact form may set cookies in your browser and read information from it, for the sole purpose of distinguishing people from bots. You can block those cookies in your browser, but the form will then not be submittable — in that case please email hello@webdev-lou.com directly instead.

Affiliate link. The Hostinger badge in the footer is a referral link. If you follow it, Hostinger may record that the visit came from this site and may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy, and I may receive a commission if you buy something. Nothing you enter on this website is passed to them.

8. Your rights as a data subject

Sections 16 to 18 of RA 10173 give you the following rights over your personal data:

  • To be informed — to know that your personal data is being or will be processed, and why. This policy is how I meet that.
  • To object — to refuse the processing, including where it is based on consent, at any time.
  • To access — to be given a copy of the personal data I hold about you, along with the details of how it has been processed and who it was disclosed to.
  • To rectification — to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • To erasure or blocking — to have your data removed or withheld from further processing where it is incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, no longer necessary, or used for a purpose you did not authorise.
  • To damages — to be indemnified for damage suffered because of inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false or unlawfully obtained use of your data.
  • To data portability — to obtain your data in a structured, commonly used, electronic format.
  • To lodge a complaint — with me, and with the National Privacy Commission. See section 14.

Under Section 17, these rights — other than the right to object — may be exercised by your lawful heirs and assigns after your death or incapacity.

How to exercise any of these rights

Email hello@webdev-lou.com with "Data Privacy Request" in the subject line, and tell me which right you are exercising and enough detail to find your record — normally the email address you used and roughly when you sent the message.

I may need to verify your identity first, so that I do not hand your data to someone else. I will respond as quickly as I can, and in any case within 30 calendar days of receiving a complete request. There is no charge; if a request is manifestly unfounded or repetitive I may charge a reasonable fee, and I will tell you before I do.

9. How your data is protected

Section 20 of RA 10173 requires reasonable and appropriate organisational, physical and technical measures. The ones in place for this site are:

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS, with HTTP Strict Transport Security enforced.
  • The enquiry record is stored outside the public web root, so no URL can reach it, and with file permissions restricted to the site's own account.
  • API secrets are stored outside the web root and are never committed to the site's public code repository.
  • The contact form is protected by rate limiting, a hidden bot trap and Google reCAPTCHA, which fails closed — if verification cannot be completed, the submission is rejected rather than let through.
  • Submitted values are validated and length-capped, and are stripped of the control characters used for mail-header and log injection.
  • The site sends a strict Content Security Policy and a set of hardening headers that limit what can execute on the page and where the form can post to.
  • Access to the enquiry record is limited to me. No one else is given a copy.

No method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security — but these measures are kept under review and updated as needed.

10. Personal data breaches

The notification will describe what happened, what data was involved, what I am doing about it, and what you can do to protect yourself.
